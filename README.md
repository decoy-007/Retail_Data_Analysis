Real-World Sales Analytics & Business Intelligence

 Project Overview

This project simulates a real-world data analyst assessment involving a messy sales dataset containing inconsistent, incomplete, and potentially erroneous data.

The goal was to transform raw transactional data into reliable business insights using **Microsoft SQL Server** and **Power BI**.

The project follows a realistic analytics workflow:

> **Raw Data → Data Quality Checks → SQL Data Cleaning → Exploratory Analysis → Power BI Data Model → DAX Analysis → Interactive Dashboard → Business Recommendations**

The analysis focuses on sales performance, product profitability, customer behaviour, marketing campaigns, sales channels, and operational data quality.

---

Business Objective

The objective of this project was to answer key business questions such as:

- How is the business performing over time?
- Which products and cities generate the most revenue?
- Which products are the most profitable?
- Who are the most valuable customers?
- How many customers are repeat customers?
- Which sales channels perform best?
- Which marketing campaigns generate the most revenue and profit?
- How do discounts affect revenue and profitability?
- Where are cancellations and returns concentrated?
- Are there data quality issues that could affect business decisions?
